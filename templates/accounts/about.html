About Healio

Healthcare, connected.

Healio is a centralized medical system designed to provide seamless access to healthcare services. Our platform connects patients with top-rated doctors and hospitals, making it easier to book appointments, consult specialists, and manage health records.

We aim to improve accessibility and efficiency in healthcare by leveraging technology, so that patients, doctors, pharmacies and laboratories can work from the same, up-to-date record.

Patients

Find a doctor, book a slot and keep prescriptions, tests and history together.

Doctors

Manage your schedule, see your appointments and update patient records.

Pharmacies & labs

Look up prescriptions and track the tests that doctors request.
